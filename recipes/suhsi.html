Classic Sushi

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 sheets nori
  • 1 avocado (sliced)
  • 8 oz smoked salmon (sliced)
  • 1 cucumber (julienned)

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice

    Rinse the sushi rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Cook according to package instructions. Once cooked, transfer to a large bowl and let it cool.

  2. Prepare the seasoning

    In a small bowl, mix the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Gently fold the seasoning into the cooled rice.

  3. Prepare the fillings

    Slice the avocado and salmon into thin strips. Julienne the cucumber.

  4. Assemble the sushi

    Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo mat. Spread a thin layer of seasoned rice over the nori. Add your desired fillings in a line across the center. Roll tightly using the mat.
